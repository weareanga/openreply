Data Deletion Instructions — ANGA OpenReply

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ANGA OpenReply processes Instagram interaction data (usernames, user IDs, comment and message text) for people who interact with the @weareanga Instagram account, as described in our privacy policy.

How to request deletion

Send an email to hello@weareanga.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include your Instagram username. We will delete the interaction logs associated with your account (comment and message records held by the tool) and confirm completion by email within 30 days.

What is deleted

All records the tool holds that are linked to your Instagram user ID or username: logged comments, direct-message automation records and tracked-link click records. Data held by Meta (for example, the comments themselves on Instagram) is governed by Meta's own tools and policies.
