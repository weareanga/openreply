Terms of Service — ANGA OpenReply

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ANGA OpenReply is a software tool operated by ANGA for managing automated replies on ANGA's own Instagram account, @weareanga, through Meta's Instagram Platform APIs.

Use

The tool is used only by ANGA's team, only on ANGA's own Instagram account. It sends automated public replies and direct messages in response to comments and messages initiated by people who interact with the account (for example, commenting a keyword to receive product information or a promotional code).

Acceptable use

The tool operates within Meta's Platform Terms and Instagram's terms. It does not send unsolicited bulk messages, does not message people who have not interacted with the account, and honours opt-outs: anyone who asks not to receive messages will not be contacted again by the automation.

Liability

The tool is provided as is, for ANGA's internal use. Promotional codes distributed through the tool are governed by the terms of each promotion as stated in the message that delivers them.
