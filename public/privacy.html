Privacy Policy — ANGA OpenReply

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ANGA OpenReply is an internal tool operated by ANGA (weareanga.com) to manage automated replies on ANGA's own Instagram account, @weareanga. This policy explains what data the tool processes and why.

What data we process

When a person interacts with the @weareanga Instagram account, the tool may process: their Instagram username and user ID, the text of comments they post on @weareanga content, the text of direct messages they send to @weareanga, and timestamps of those interactions. We do not collect data from people who do not interact with the account, and we do not buy or receive data from data brokers.

Why we process it

Solely to operate the account's automated and manual replies: matching keywords in comments (for example, a product name or a promotional code word), sending the requested information by direct message, replying publicly to comments, and keeping a log so the team can follow up and prevent duplicate messages.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed on our infrastructure providers (hosting, database and queue services) only as needed to run the tool, and via Meta's Instagram Platform APIs under Meta's terms.

Retention

Interaction logs are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and are deleted on request. Promotional campaign logs are reviewed and cleared after each campaign ends.

Your rights and deletion

You may ask what data we hold about your Instagram account and ask for its deletion at any time. See our data deletion page for instructions, or contact us at hello@weareanga.com. We respond within 30 days.
